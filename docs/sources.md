# Sources

The guides cite chapter, section and page numbers so you can read a few dozen pages instead of a whole book. This page gives the full bibliographic details, plus what is and is not in the repository.

> **The PDFs are not here.** Textbooks, lecturer notes and past papers are copyrighted and are not redistributed. They are in the shared course Drive folder linked from the [root README](../README.md). Page numbers in the guides refer to those copies; a different edition may shift by a few pages.

## Official and standards documents

- **Benchmark Minimum Academic Standards (BMAS), Mechatronics Engineering** — the official synopsis quoted at the top of each guide. Copy supplied by the department; check the current edition.
- **FUTO project guidelines** — proposal, report and defence format for MCE 515. Confirm the current edition with your supervisor.
- **SIWES / industrial-training guidelines** — logbook, supervisor assessment and report format for MCE 529.
- **IEEE citation style** — [Purdue OWL overview](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/research_and_citation/ieee_style/ieee_overview.html) is the reference used in the writing guides.

## Open online resources

These are free and were checked when the guides were written. Use them as backup, not as a replacement for the cited chapter.

- [Modern Robotics](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/) — videos and the free textbook preprint (MCE 501).
- [MIT 6.003: Signals and Systems](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-003-signals-and-systems-fall-2011/) — z-transform, DTFT/DFT, sampling (MCE 503).
- [The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to DSP](https://www.dspguide.com/) — practical ADC, FFT and filter chapters (MCE 503).
- [MIT 2.003SC: Engineering Dynamics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003sc-engineering-dynamics-fall-2011/) — vibration and MDOF units (MCE 507).
- [RealPars](https://www.realpars.com/blog/) — short PLC, SCADA, fieldbus and motor-starter articles (MCE 511, 513).
- [MIT 6.777J: Design and Fabrication of MEMS](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-777j-design-and-fabrication-of-microelectromechanical-devices-spring-2007/) — the microfabrication sequence (MCE 521).
- [Purdue OWL: IEEE style](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/research_and_citation/ieee_style/ieee_overview.html) — citations and references (MCE 515, 529).

## A note on editions

Where the Drive copy is a scan of an older edition, the page numbers follow that scan. If you buy a newer edition, use the section headings in the guide rather than the page numbers. The chapter titles are stable even when the numbering is not.
