# Past papers

The guides refer to past questions by paper and number. This page explains the shorthand and lists what was available for each course.

> **The papers are not in this repository.** They are department material. Get them from the shared course Drive folder (linked in the [root README](../README.md)) or from a course mate. The guides cite question numbers only; they do not reproduce the text.

## Shorthand

| Written as | Means |
|---|---|
| `2020/21 Q3(a)` | 2020/2021 session, question 3, part (a) |
| `2021/22 Sec B Q2` | 2021/2022 session, Section B, question 2 |
| `2019/20 Q1–Q2` | 2019/2020 session, questions 1 to 2 |
| `AOC` | The lecturer's AOC paper (department copy), used for MCE 511 and 513 |

Question numbers follow the printed paper. Where a scan is partial or the numbering is unclear, the guide says "partial" or omits the part letter rather than guessing.

## How to use a paper

1. Read the question and identify the topic from the guide's **Exam** line.
2. Attempt it with the book closed, under time.
3. Mark against the textbook method, not against a memorised answer.
4. If a question spans two topics, note both; that is how the papers combine ideas.

## A caution

Three sessions is a small sample. A topic that has not appeared for two years can return, especially when the lecturer changes. Use the frequency labels to order your revision, not to skip the synopsis.
